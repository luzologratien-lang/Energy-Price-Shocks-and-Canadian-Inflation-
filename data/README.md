# Data Sources
This analysis is based on four monthly Canadian macroeconomic variables covering the period from January 1999 to December 2025:

- Energy Prices: is retrieved from Statistics Canada, Table 18-10-0004-01 (formerly CANSIM 326-0020).

- Headline and Core Inflation: are both obtained from Bank of Canada’s Key Inflation Indicators dashboard.

- Policy Rate: Bank of Canada’s overnight rate is obtained from FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), series IRSTCB01CAM156N.

After collecting the data from these sources, I cleaned, harmonized, and combined them into a single dataset.
